## 2. Article type, length, language
- Type: **Research Article** (S1: "Present original findings, new developments, or critical
  reflections... clearly state the research question, methodology, and results"; suggested length
  **12–25 pages**).
- Language: English is acceptable (S1 accepts Portuguese/Spanish/English, per journal scope page S6).
- Initial submission: **PDF formatted in LaTeX**; LaTeX source required at final version (S1, S2).
- S1: "While the initial submission doesn't require strict adherence to RITA's page size, please
  observe the following structure and guidelines." We nevertheless target the 12–25 page range.

## 3. Required sequence and formatting (S1, S2)
1. Title
2. Author names (with affiliations and emails **in a footnote**)
3. Abstract (**<= 200 words**) and Keywords
4. Text (including figures and tables)
5. Acknowledgments (if applicable)
6. References

Additional checklist items (S2): single-spaced; **12-point font**; italics instead of underlining
(except URLs); figures and tables placed within the text; URLs included for all references whenever
available; title concise/informative; abstract presents objectives, methodology, results,
conclusions; keywords relevant; clear methodology/results/discussion/conclusion; complete author
information; conflict-of-interest declaration; free of plagiarism.

## 4. Figures, tables, references (S1)
- Figures and tables numbered consecutively, placed near their citation; figure titles in the text
  (caption), not inside the image.
- References **numbered and cited in brackets**, following the template's examples. (Template uses
  `abntex2cite` with `[num]` and `\citebrackets[]`; verified in §6 test build.)
- URLs for all references whenever available (S2). Our `refs.bib` carries `url`/`urldate` for gray
  literature and DOI for peer-reviewed entries.

## 5. AI policy (S3) — binding requirements mapped to our manuscript
| Requirement (S3) | How satisfied |
|---|---|
| AI cannot be author/co-author | Single human author; AI use only as declared tool |
| Authors assume full responsibility incl. hallucination/plagiarism/bias | Author review; every claim traceable to `results/` or verified reference |
| AI may assist formatting/editing/translation/readability only, not replace analytical work | Declaration section states scope of AI assistance; analytical decisions, model and proofs owned by author |
| **Mandatory** section titled exactly **"Declaration of AI-Assisted Use in Writing"** immediately **before References** | `main.tex` has `\section*{Declaration of AI-Assisted Use in Writing}` placed directly before `\bibliography` |
| AI-generated primary data/figures prohibited | All data from executed experiments; figures generated by deterministic scripts from CSVs |

## 6. Template: what we use, and conflicts resolved
- The official link S4 is dead (404) as of 2026-11-24. We use the archived official archive (S5);
  provenance is recorded here and the class is committed as `SelfArx.cls`.
- The template is a two-column, 10 pt `SelfArx` class (derived from LaTeX Templates "Stylish
  Article") with RITA macros: `\JournalInfo`, `\Archive`, `\PaperTitle`, `\ShortTitle`, `\Titulo`
  (Portuguese title), `\PaperVol`, `\PaperNum`, `\PaperAno`, `\Authors`, `\affiliation`,
  `\Keywords`, `\PalavrasChave`, `\Abstract`, `\Resumo`, `\Doi`, `\DateR`, `\DateA`; citations via
  `abntex2cite` numeric style; references heading "References".
- **Conflict:** template example uses 10 pt; written checklist (S2) requires 12 pt, single-spaced.
  Per the author's instruction ("if the template's example PDF conflicts with the written
  guidelines, the written guidelines win"), we compile the official class with the **12 pt option**
  and single spacing. The two-column layout, RITA header/footer, bilingual title/abstract block and
  numeric bracketed citations are retained.
- Template's bilingual block (`\Titulo`, `\Resumo`, `\PalavrasChave`) is filled with an accurate
  Portuguese translation (translation assistance is declared under S3's allowed uses).
- DOI/Received/Accepted lines are left with the template's placeholder pattern until the venue
  assigns them; the corresponding-author line carries the author's name and e-mail.
